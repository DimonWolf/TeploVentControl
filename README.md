<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="date"><strong>Effective date:</strong> September 13, 2026</p>

<p>
This Privacy Policy describes how <strong>Modbus485</strong> ("the App"),
developed by <strong>TeploVentControl</strong>, handles information when you
use the application.
</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>

<p>
The App is designed to communicate with external hardware devices using the
USB Host functionality of your Android device.
</p>

<p>
<strong>The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information.</strong>
</p>

<p>The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Name or surname</li>
    <li>Email address</li>
    <li>Phone number</li>
    <li>Precise or approximate location</li>
    <li>Contacts</li>
    <li>Photos or videos</li>
    <li>Audio recordings</li>
    <li>Financial or payment information</li>
    <li>Health information</li>
    <li>Advertising identifiers</li>
    <li>Personal identification information</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. USB Device Access</h2>

<p>
The App may access USB devices connected to your Android device.
</p>

<p>
USB communication is used solely to allow the App to communicate with
compatible external hardware, including Modbus/RS-485 devices.
</p>

<p>
The App does not upload USB device data to our servers or share USB
communication data with third parties.
</p>

<p>
Android may display a system permission dialog when the App requests access
to a connected USB device. USB access is granted by the user through the
Android operating system.
</p>

<h2>3. Data Storage</h2>

<p>
The App does not operate a remote server and does not maintain a database
containing personal information.
</p>

<p>
Any data temporarily processed by the App is used only for the operation of
the application and communication with the connected hardware.
</p>

<h2>4. Data Sharing</h2>

<p>
We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.
</p>

<p>
The App does not use personal information for advertising or profiling.
</p>

<h2>5. Third-Party Services</h2>

<p>
The App does not use third-party advertising or analytics services that
collect personal information.
</p>

<p>
Google Play may independently collect information in accordance with
Google's own privacy policies and terms. Such collection is controlled by
Google and is not performed by the App.
</p>

<h2>6. Children's Privacy</h2>

<p>
The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or
any other users.
</p>

<p>
Because the App does not collect personal information, no personal data is
knowingly collected from children under the applicable age of consent.
</p>

<h2>7. Security</h2>

<p>
Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information to our
servers, there is no personal information maintained by us on a remote
server.
</p>

<p>
Users should nevertheless protect their Android device and connected
hardware using appropriate security measures.
</p>

<h2>8. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>

<p>
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
</p>

<p>
Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" at the top
of this Privacy Policy.
</p>
